📖 Project Overview
MindGuard is an AI-powered study burnout detection and student wellness platform designed to help university students maintain a healthier academic lifestyle by identifying early signs of burnout before they become severe.

The project was developed as part of the Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) course during the 4th Semester of Software Engineering.

Unlike traditional productivity applications that focus only on task completion, MindGuard emphasizes student well-being, balancing productivity with mental health through behavioral analysis, wellness tracking, and user-centered design.

Rather than functioning as only a prototype, the project represents a complete UX Case Study, covering every major stage of the Human-Centered Design process—from user research to interactive high-fidelity interfaces.

🎯 Problem Statement
University students frequently experience academic burnout due to:

📚 Continuous academic pressure
⏰ Poor time management
😴 Lack of healthy study habits
😟 Increased stress and anxiety
📉 Reduced motivation
⚠️ Difficulty recognizing early burnout symptoms
Most existing productivity applications help students organize tasks but fail to recognize the user's emotional and mental condition.

MindGuard was designed to bridge this gap by combining productivity features with AI-assisted wellness monitoring.

💡 Proposed Solution
MindGuard introduces a student-centered platform capable of monitoring study behavior and encouraging healthier academic routines.

The proposed solution combines:

🤖 AI-assisted burnout prediction (conceptual)
📊 Study pattern monitoring
😊 Mood tracking
🎯 Goal management
📅 Smart study scheduling
⏳ Focus sessions
📈 Progress tracking
🌱 Wellness recommendations
🔔 Intelligent reminders
Instead of simply helping students study longer, MindGuard encourages them to study smarter while maintaining their mental well-being.

🔍 User Research
Before designing the interface, research was conducted to understand how students experience academic burnout.

The research focused on identifying:

Common stress factors
Study habits
Productivity challenges
Time management issues
Mental wellness concerns
Existing application limitations
The collected findings formed the foundation of the entire design process and influenced every major design decision throughout the project.

📊 Research Insights
Some of the most significant observations included:

Students frequently overwork during examination periods.
Many users struggle to balance productivity and self-care.
Existing productivity tools rarely monitor emotional well-being.
Students prefer simple interfaces with minimal cognitive load.
Personalized feedback increases engagement.
Preventive wellness support is more valuable than reactive solutions.
These insights guided the overall information architecture and interaction design of MindGuard.
